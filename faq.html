01 What is Boomerang?

Boomerang is a conversational AI platform created for modern financial institutions. Its AI agents communicate with borrowers across email, SMS and voice while connecting with the systems institutions already use.

02 Which parts of the borrower lifecycle can it support?

Boomerang can support customer onboarding, payment reminders, loan servicing, borrower assistance, collections and follow-up communication throughout the borrower lifecycle.

03 Which communication channels are available?

The platform supports conversations through email, SMS and voice. Institutions can create consistent experiences across channels while maintaining the context of each borrower conversation.

04 Does Boomerang integrate with existing systems?

Yes. Boomerang is designed to connect with existing servicing platforms, customer relationship systems and operational tools so institutions do not need to replace their current infrastructure.

05 How does Boomerang approach compliance?

Its agents operate within institution-defined policies, record their actions and provide visibility into how conversations are handled. This helps teams maintain oversight in regulated environments.

06 Can institutions control what the AI says?

Yes. Institutions can define communication rules, escalation paths, approved information and operational boundaries. Human teams retain control over how agents interact with borrowers.
